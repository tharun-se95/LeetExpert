# The Grid Underneath as the app's dark theme, and Grid family hues in both themes

Status: approved by owner and implemented 2026-09-25 · branch `feat/grid-app-theme`

## 1. Owner direction

"Take all from The Grid Underneath artifact" (`web/lesson-artifacts/dist/_grid-underneath.html`,
its tokens in `web/lesson-artifacts/shared.py` `GRID_DARK`). Owner chose, 2026-09-25:

- the Grid's **navy ladder** and **navy code well** for the app's dark theme;
- the Grid's **blue accent** on buttons, focus rings and header in dark;
- the Grid's **dot grid** on the dark page;
- **family colours** replaced by the Grid's state hues, **in both themes**;
- a **7th Grid-style hue** for the family the Grid has no hue for (answer 2026-09-25).

This reverses three CLAUDE.md §4 rules for the dark theme: "primary is steel everywhere",
"the desk is flat paper" and the space-black page (owner 2026-09-19). They were overruled on
purpose; this spec does not re-argue them. The light theme keeps its paper ladder, steel chrome
and flat desk. Only its family hues change.

**Assumed, not asked:** status colours (good/bad/warn/info/insight, generated by
`lib/visual/palette.ts`) and callout tints stay generated in both themes. The owner was offered
moving them to Grid hues and did not pick it. If that changes, it is a follow-up.

## 3. Dark chrome accent

In `.dark` the Grid blue is split as the Grid splits it (`--acc` fill, `--acc-ink` text):
`--press-lime` (`--pop`, CTA and chip fills) is the fill blue `#5C8FFF` (≥3:1 on every dark
step, worst 4.29 on raise); `--press-olive` (`--accent`) and `--press-blue` (`--mark`, the
wordmark) are the text blue `#8DB0FF` (6.14 on raise, 4.93 on the navy card's lifted step).
*Found in implementation:* `--accent` cannot be the fill blue, because `text-accent` sets text in
32 places and `paperContrast.test.ts` holds it to AA. `--on-pop` is `#0E1726` (5.84:1 on
`#5C8FFF`). Focus rings follow `--accent`. Hover `#A9C4FF`, active `#7AA2FF`, both measured.

The brand lamp stays grass green and stays the mark's alone. What changes is the CLAUDE.md
wording "the one non-steel ink in chrome": in dark it is the one non-blue ink in chrome.

## 4. Dot grid (dark only)

`.dark` paints the desk (the page paper under the one desk wrapper, never cards, sheets, code,
IDE or dialogs, which stay solid) with the Grid's dots:
`radial-gradient(#202C3A 1.4px, transparent 1.4px)` at `26px 26px`.
`paperContrast.test.ts` holds muted text to AA over a dot as well as over the paper.
The translucent header over scrolled content is measured over a dot too.

## 5. Family hues, both themes

Seven families, seven hues. Dark = the Grid hex. Light = the same OKLCH hue, lightness (then
chroma) lowered until **white text on it clears 4.5:1**. That also puts it ≥3.31:1 on all four
light surfaces, so one light value serves both fills and chrome (`uiAccent` has nothing to push).

| Family | Today | Dark (Grid) | Light (derived) | Ink on dark fill | Ink on light fill |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| linear-traversal | `#0A7A6A` | teal `#3CC3AE` | `#008575` | `#0E1726` 8.22 | white 4.55 |
| pointer-movement | `#C45C26` | orange `#F08A4B` | `#bc5b11` | 7.22 | 4.52 |
| ordering-search | `#2F6FED` | blue `#5C8FFF` | `#4171de` | 5.84 | 4.52 |
| recursive-exploration | `#6B4CE6` | violet `#9B86FF` | `#7b63d9` | 6.20 | 4.54 |
| state-transition | `#C9A227` | yellow `#E3BD3F` | `#907300` | 9.94 | 4.53 |
| relationships | `#1F9D8A` | **magenta `#E07BE0`** (new) | `#b14fb2` | 6.91 | 4.53 |
| priority-structures | `#E11D48` | pink `#FF6B8B` | `#d14165` | 6.61 | 4.51 |

Light values are what `lightVariant()` produces (gamut mapping in `palette.ts`); every one is
≥3.31:1 on all four light surfaces.

**The 7th hue.** Relationships and linear-traversal both sat on teal. Magenta `#E07BE0`
(OKLCH L0.73 C0.176 h327) is in the Grid's band (L0.67-0.81, C0.12-0.18) and was chosen by
measured distance: 0.120 ΔE-OK from its nearest Grid hue (violet), more than blue and violet
are from each other (0.080, the closest pair; the test floor is 0.075). Teal is the family
nearest the lamp, at 0.125; the floor is 0.1. It is 0.37 from the lamp green and 0.39 from `--good`, and reads 5.07:1
on the raise. Lime and sky candidates fell within 0.06-0.10 of the lamp, teal or yellow.

**Mechanism.** Today `familyCssVars` writes one hex as inline style for both themes, so a family
cannot differ per theme. Proposed:

- `:root { color-scheme: light }` and `.dark { color-scheme: dark }`. This also darkens native
  scrollbars and form controls in dark, which is correct there. Check the IDE and the sandbox
  inputs in the browser.
- `familyTheme.ts` authors **one Grid hex per family**. The light value is derived (a pure
  function, tested), and `onAccent` is computed per theme by `pickInk`, no longer authored.
- `familyCssVars` emits `--family-accent: light-dark(<light>, <dark>)` etc. It is resolved where
  the colour is used, so a `dark navy-card` inside the light theme gets the dark hue, which is
  right on navy.
- JS consumers that paint a raw hex where CSS functions do not resolve (SVG attributes, canvas,
  video brand files) are audited one by one: `HeroFloor`, `ReviewerAvatar`, `Diagram`,
  `VizPlayer`, `PatternLab`, `DecisionObservatory`, the course hub and module cards, the problem
  filters, `lib/visual/hyperframesBrand.ts`. Each either moves to a CSS variable or picks the
  variant for the surface it paints on. None may keep reading the old single `accent`.

**Callout tints (found in implementation).** On the navy card, the generated level-1 hues are
as dark as the card (a 100% mix steps only 1.11), so the dark tints mix level 2 at 40%
(advice's stand-in `#3A4A60` at 50%). Steps are 1.18-1.29, labels ≥4.88, body ≥8.53.
