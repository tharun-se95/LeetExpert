# SEO Strategy

## Position

leetExpert is a DSA course where every concept has a visualization, a live
Python **and** JavaScript sandbox, and solve-first problems. 220 authored
lessons across 24 modules.

## The honest competitive picture (SERPs sampled 2026-09-09)

Two distinct battlegrounds, with very different odds:

**1. "DSA visualizer" / "DSA course" head terms — do not contest.**
Crowded with established tool-sites and course aggregators (programiz.com,
visualizedsa.com, dsaviz.com, dsavisualizer.in, learndsa.org, Coursera). A
one-day-old domain has no path here in the near term. Chasing these is how
the whole programme wastes its first year.

**2. Pattern and concept explanations — contest these.**
Queries like "sliding window pattern explained" are dominated by **LeetCode
Discuss threads and Medium posts**. That is a weak incumbent set: Discuss
threads are unstructured and undated, Medium posts are frequently
member-only paywalled, and almost all of them are single-language (usually
Java or C++). A well-structured, permanently-free, dual-language lesson with
a working sandbox is genuinely a better answer to that query, not just a
differently-marketed one.

## The wedge

**Dual-language + runnable.** Nearly every competing pattern explainer forces
one language. leetExpert's lessons carry Python *and* JavaScript reference
solutions that are executed in CI. That maps directly onto real long-tail
demand ("... in python", "... in javascript") that the incumbents split.

## What we do

1. **Win long-tail pattern queries first.** Concept lessons already exist for
   these; the job is making them findable and better-targeted, not writing new
   pages.
2. **Build topical authority within DSA before widening.** 24 modules is
   already a real cluster structure; strengthen the internal links between
   concept lessons and the problems that exercise them.
3. **Earn links with tools, not outreach.** The visualizations and the sandbox
   are inherently linkable; a standalone, embeddable complexity/visualizer
   surface would be the strongest backlink asset the codebase can produce.

## What we explicitly do not do

- No programmatic page generation. The obvious template ("`{problem}` solution
  in `{language}`" × 200 × 2) would produce ~400 near-duplicate pages of
  content that already exists on the problem pages. That is doorway-page
  behaviour and it would put the whole domain at risk.
- No head-term chasing while the domain has no authority.
- No content velocity for its own sake. 220 lessons already exist and are not
  yet indexed; writing more before the existing set ranks is misallocation.

## Sequencing

| Horizon | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Now | Get indexed. Search Console, sitemap submission, analytics. |
| Weeks 1–6 | Let the 247 URLs be crawled. Do not churn. Watch which queries Google chooses to associate with the site. |
| Weeks 6–12 | Act on real query data: title/description tuning for pages at positions 4–20. |
| Month 3+ | Content gaps and link-worthy tooling, informed by data rather than guesses. |
