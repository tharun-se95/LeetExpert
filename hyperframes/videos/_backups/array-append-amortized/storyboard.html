Appending isn't always O(1)

storyboard v1 1920×1080 · ~40s · 8 beats · concept-explainer with process

A dynamic array's append is amortized O(1): one push into a full array copies every element, and doubling is what keeps that cheap on average. Every number here is computed by sources/compute.py (mirrored in compute.mjs) — none typed by hand.
