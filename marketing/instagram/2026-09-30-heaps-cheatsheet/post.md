## Alt text (per slide)

**Slide 1.** Cover. A leetExpert cheatsheet titled "Heaps", with a "Save this" tag. Tagline: "Priority in O(log n) — top-K and running medians live here." Chips list what is inside: 4 smells to patterns, 4 patterns, 3 targets and 3 traps.

**Slide 2.** Smell to pattern. Repeated extract-min/max → Binary heap. Top K in a stream → Size-K heap. Merge K sorted → Heap of heads. Cooldown / gap scheduling → Greedy with heap.

**Slide 3.** Pattern 1 of 4: Size-K heap. When: Top K of a stream / array. For top K largest, keep a min-heap of size K. Evict the smallest when over capacity — heap root is the threshold.

**Slide 4.** Pattern 2 of 4: Two-heap median. When: Running median as values arrive. Max-heap for the lower half, min-heap for the upper. Rebalance sizes so the median sits at the boundary.

**Slide 5.** Pattern 3 of 4: K-way merge. When: Merge K sorted lists / arrays. Push the next item from each list with its list id. Pop global min, push that list’s successor.

**Slide 6.** Pattern 4 of 4: Greedy with heap (cooldown). When: Task scheduler / spaced gaps. Always run the task with the largest remaining count; park just-run tasks in a cooldown queue until their gap clears. A plain queue drifts from optimal.

**Slide 7.** Targets, time and extra space. Push / pop: O(log n) time, O(n) space (n = heap size). Top-K over stream: O(n log k) time, O(k) space (Better than full sort). K-way merge: O(n log k) time, O(k) space (n total items, k lists). Traps. Min vs max heap mix-up: Top-K largest needs a min-heap of size K (evict small). Flipping the heap type silently inverts the answer. Comparing tuples incorrectly: When the heap stores (value, index, …), define the full comparison. Partial compares break ties and corrupt order. Cooldown queue vs heap state: A task on cooldown must re-enter the heap, not stay parked. Forgetting to re-push is how task-scheduler counts come out too small.

**Slide 8.** Closing slide: "The real Heaps cheatsheet, from the course." One of 21 cheatsheets in leetExpert, the interactive DSA course, each under its module's practice problems. leetexpert.com, link in bio.
