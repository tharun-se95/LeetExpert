## Alt text (per slide)

**Slide 1.** Cover. A leetExpert cheatsheet titled "Recursion & Backtracking", with a "Save this" tag. Tagline: "Choose → explore → unchoose. The call stack is your state." Chips list what is inside: 4 smells to patterns, 4 patterns, 2 targets and 3 traps.

**Slide 2.** Smell to pattern. All subsets / permutations → Backtracking tree. Constraint board (N-Queens…) → Place + prune. Divide structure in half → Recurse on parts. Every valid arrangement / path → Enumerate + record.

**Slide 3.** Pattern 1 of 4: Backtracking template. When: Enumerate valid configurations. Push a choice, recurse, pop. Record when a complete valid state is reached. Prune early when constraints break.

**Slide 4.** Pattern 2 of 4: Include / exclude. When: Subsets / combination sum. For subsets: branch on taking nums[i] or not, then advance i. Same tree, two edges.

**Slide 5.** Pattern 3 of 4: Swap permutations. When: All orderings of a sequence. For i…n, swap i with j≥i, recurse i+1, swap back. Generates each order once.

**Slide 6.** Pattern 4 of 4: Place + prune (constraints). When: N-Queens, Sudoku, safe placement. Check constraints before recursing, not after recording. Pruning at the placement step is what keeps board searches from degenerating into full enumeration.

**Slide 7.** Targets, time and extra space. Subsets: O(2ⁿ · n) time, O(n) space (Output-sensitive). Permutations: O(n! · n) time, O(n) space (Depth-n stack). Traps. Mutating shared arrays: Push a copy into the answer (or copy on record). Otherwise every result points at the same final list. Forgetting to unchoose: After recurse, undo the mutation (pop, unmark, swap back). Missing undo corrupts every sibling branch. Pruning after the record: If you record partial answers and only reject them later, you still walk the dead branches. Reject at the choice, not at the leaf.

**Slide 8.** Closing slide: "The real Recursion & Backtracking cheatsheet, from the course." One of 21 cheatsheets in leetExpert, the interactive DSA course, each under its module's practice problems. leetexpert.com, link in bio.
